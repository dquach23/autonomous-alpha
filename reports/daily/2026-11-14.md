- ARK: the daily trades page on ark-funds.com.

**Framework to apply once you have the deltas**
1. **Concentration:** Count how many of the funds added the same name or theme. 3 or more is a signal. Weight it by each position's share of that fund's portfolio, not by dollar size.
2. **Selling or hedging:** Check for puts in the 13F (Burry and Tepper have used them). Look for exits of crowded mega-cap tech or AI names. Note that a 13F shows only long positions and the put and call lines. It does not show shorts.
3. **Defensive rotation:** Look for Berkshire's cash and T-bill pile growing, and for new positions in GLD, TLT, or staples. Treat any one fund as weak evidence. A pattern across several funds is the real signal.
4. **Time lag:** 13Fs are about 45 days stale. A position may have changed since the quarter ended on Sep 30.
